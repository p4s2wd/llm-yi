The History of Translation Machines

Translation machines have evolved dramatically over the past seventy years, from rule-based systems to large language models running on local hardware.

Early systems relied on dictionaries and hand-written grammar rules, which often produced awkward and unnatural sentences.

Modern Approaches

  • Statistical machine translation dominated the 2000s.
  • Neural models took over after 2014.
  • Today, large language models can preserve tone and style.

Running a model locally protects privacy, because no text ever leaves the user's own machine.
